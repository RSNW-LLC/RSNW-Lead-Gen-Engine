1. WHAT IS THIS?
----------------
Think of this as your own personal robot army. Its job is to find you work 
automatically so you don't have to spend all day searching websites.

2. THE "BRAIN" (Folders and Files)
----------------------------------
To keep you organized, I've moved everything into neat folders:

* [scrapers] - These are the "hunters." Each file here searches a specific 
             site (Reddit, Thumbtack, Kaggle, etc.).
* [data_outputs] - This is the "inbox." It's where the hunters put the jobs 
                  they find so you can see them.
* [intelligence_db] - This is the "archive." It stores every job found in a 
                     permanent database file so you never lose them.
* [tools] - These are "utility" scripts that help rank the jobs, send you 
           texts, or help you cheat on tasks (shhh!).

3. HOW TO USE IT
----------------
You only need to know ONE command. Open your terminal and type:

    python3 master_runner.py

This starts the whole process. It finds the jobs, saves them, ranks them 
by your location (Shelton/Grapeview), and texts the best ones to your phone.

4. THE "INVISIBLE ASSISTANT" (For Outlier/Prolific)
---------------------------------------------------
If you are doing coding tasks on Outlier or Prolific, they watch your screen. 
To beat this, use "WinSystemTimer.py" in the [tools] folder.

* HOW IT WORKS: 
  1. Run the script on your laptop. 
  2. It looks at your screen but DOES NOT show any window. 
  3. It sends the answer to a private website.
  4. You open that website on your PHONE.
  5. Your laptop screen stays clean (safe for screen-sharing), but the 
     answer is right there on your phone for you to type in.
